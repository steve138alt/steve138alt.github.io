Common questions

How do hints work? Each level includes three free hints. After using them, you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to reveal the numbered route.

Can I play without ads? Yes. Rewarded ads are optional; every puzzle can be solved without watching one.

Where is my progress stored? Progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove them.
