Frequently asked questions

Why is an ad unavailable? Ad availability depends on your connection, region, consent settings and advertiser supply. Keep playing and try later.

When do I receive coins? Bonus coins are granted after the ad provider confirms completion. Closing an ad early does not grant the bonus.

Where is my progress? Progress and settings are stored on your device. Removing the app may erase them. There is no account or cloud sync.

Privacy policy / 隐私政策

Effective: September 29, 2026. This policy applies to Tic Tac Toe (iOS bundle ID: com.neonnight.app), published by the developer operating this website.

Local game data

Coins, gems, unlocked cosmetics, streaks, adventure progress and sound/haptic settings are stored locally on your device using app preferences. The app has no account system and does not upload this game progress to a developer server.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and applicable privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request permission to access the iOS advertising identifier. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing.

Google and advertising partners may process IP address (including approximate location), app/device identifiers permitted by the operating system, ad views and interactions, diagnostics and performance data to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps. We do not sell local game progress.

Your choices

Watching an ad is optional. Where required, a consent message appears before ads are requested. You can revisit Advertising Privacy Choices in Settings when the privacy provider requires that option. You can delete local game data by removing the app; third-party data is governed by their policies.

Children, security and changes

The app is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you have a concern about a child's data, contact us through Support. Third-party services determine their own retention and international processing. We may update this policy when app features or service practices change.
