SortFactory / Tiny Sort Factory

Tap, sort fruit, earn stars and build your tiny factory. Play 60 levels and unlock mascots with game coins.

Support / 技术支持

Parents and guardians can report the app version, device model and problem through developer support. Reports are public; do not include children's names, personal or sensitive information. Children should ask a parent to contact us.

Children's Privacy Policy / 儿童隐私政策

Updated September 29, 2026. Covers the child-directed version of SortFactory for iOS, developed by steve138alt, intended for children under 13.

Local game data

Progress, coins, stars, mascots and sound/haptic preferences stay on your device. No account, name, birth date, email or precise location is requested. Reset Progress resets game data; uninstalling removes local app data. Resetting game progress does not reset SDK privacy state.

Child-directed advertising

The optional Shop video ad offers 100 virtual coins after completion; closing early earns no reward. Coins have no cash value. Gameplay is available without watching ads. A parent challenge precedes ad presentation and external privacy links; this challenge is not verifiable parental consent for personal-data processing.

All Google Mobile Ads requests are configured for child age treatment, maximum G-rated ad content and disabled personalization. Publisher first-party ID is disabled. The app does not request Apple's tracking permission, access IDFA, or enable behavioral advertising or remarketing. UMP is tagged for users under the age of consent; adult European personalized-ad consent messages are suppressed for these users.

SDK data processing

Child-directed settings do not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. Google may process network information such as IP address and approximate region, other device-related information, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash, performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and operation under its child-directed restrictions. We do not provide Google with a child's name, email, birth date or game save.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google child age treatment documentation. Google may process data outside your country under its safeguards and retention practices. Advertising partner data is governed by its applicable policies; we do not claim that local Reset Progress deletes partner-held data.

Parents' choices and contact

Children can play without optional ads. Parents may reset local progress or uninstall the app. For questions, requests or concerns, parents can use the public developer support page without posting private information. We do not solicit personal information from children. If a child posts personal information in a report, a guardian should request removal without repeating that information publicly.
