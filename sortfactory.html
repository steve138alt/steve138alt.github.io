SortFactory / Tiny Sort Factory

Tap, sort fruit, earn stars and build your tiny factory. Play 60 levels and unlock mascots with game coins. A casual puzzle game for a general audience.

Support / 技术支持

Report your app version, device model and problem through developer support. Reports are public; do not post personal or sensitive information. We will help with gameplay, technical issues and privacy questions.

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated November 10, 2026. Covers SortFactory for iOS, developed by steve138alt. SortFactory is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed to children.

Local game data

Progress, coins, stars, mascots and sound/haptic preferences stay on your device. No account, name, birth date, email or precise location is required. Reset Progress resets game data; uninstalling removes local app data. Resetting progress does not reset advertising privacy choices.

Optional advertising

The Shop offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, including Meta Audience Network as a mediation partner. Completing one earns 100 virtual coins; closing early earns no reward. Coins have no cash value. Gameplay remains available without watching ads.

After collecting applicable advertising consent choices, we request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission from eligible users before enabling tracking. Personalized advertising is permitted only with the relevant consent and ATT authorization. Otherwise, Google ad requests use non-personalized settings; the advertising identifier is unavailable without ATT authorization. We retain a maximum G content rating for Google ads and disable Google's publisher first-party ID. Declining tracking does not change gameplay or the reward for completing an available ad.

SDK data processing

Non-personalized advertising does not mean no data collection. Google Mobile Ads SDK and Meta Audience Network SDK may process IP addresses and estimated general location, device-related identifiers, advertising views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and SDK operation. With the relevant consent and ATT authorization, advertising partners may link advertising identifiers and advertising interactions with information from other companies' apps and websites for personalized advertising and advertising measurement. We do not provide advertising partners with your name, email, birth date or game save.

Where required, Google's consent form appears before requesting ads. You can change available choices through Ad Privacy Choices in Settings. Advertising consent does not override an ATT refusal, and ATT authorization does not override a refusal of relevant advertising consent. Manage ATT permission in iOS Settings. Preloaded ads are discarded when relevant privacy choices change. Depending on consent, region and inventory, ads may be limited or unavailable without affecting gameplay.

Older builds may use only Google ads. Meta advertising is available in integrated builds once the mediation source is configured. The ATT flow described above applies beginning with version 2.0 build 3; older builds do not request tracking permission.

Read Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and Meta Privacy Policy. Advertising partners may process data outside your country under their safeguards and retention policies. Resetting local progress does not delete data held by advertising partners. Use each provider's privacy tools for requests concerning data held by that provider.

Your choices and contact

Reset local game data in Settings or uninstall the app. Manage available advertising privacy choices in Settings and iOS permissions through system settings. For privacy questions or requests, use developer support without posting personal information. We do not intentionally solicit personal information from children. If personal information is inadvertently posted, request removal without repeating it publicly. This policy may change with app functionality.
