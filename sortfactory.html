SortFactory / Tiny Sort Factory

Tap, sort fruit, earn stars and build your tiny factory. Play 60 levels and unlock mascots with game coins.

Support / 技术支持

Report your app version, device model and problem through developer support. Reports are public; do not include personal or sensitive information.

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective September 29, 2026. Covers SortFactory for iOS, developed by steve138alt.

Local game data

Progress, coins, stars, mascots and sound/haptic preferences are stored on your device. No account required. Reset Progress resets game data; uninstalling removes local app data. Resetting progress does not reset advertising privacy choices.

Optional ads

The Shop offers optional Google AdMob video ads. Completing one earns 100 virtual coins; closing early earns no reward. Coins have no cash value. Normal gameplay is available without watching ads.

Google data processing

Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and estimated general location, device identifiers, advertising views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, performance and diagnostic data for advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention and SDK operation. Google and its partners process data under their policies and consent choices. We do not sell game progress or provide Google with your name or email.

Where required, Google consent forms appear before ad requests. Ad Privacy Choices in Settings allows changes when required. iOS may separately ask for tracking permission. Declining does not block gameplay or rewards; the advertising identifier is unavailable without authorization.

Read Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Processing may occur outside your country under Google's safeguards and retention policies.

Choices and contact

Reset game data or manage available ad privacy choices in Settings. Manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. Contact us through the public developer support page; do not post private data. This policy may change when app functionality changes.
