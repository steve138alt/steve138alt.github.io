SortFactory / Tiny Sort Factory

Tap, sort fruit, earn stars and build your tiny factory. Play 60 levels and unlock mascots with game coins. A casual puzzle game for a general audience.

Support / 技术支持

Report your app version, device model and problem through developer support. Reports are public; do not post personal or sensitive information. We will help with gameplay, technical issues and privacy questions.

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated September 29, 2026. Covers SortFactory for iOS, developed by steve138alt. SortFactory is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed to children.

Local game data

Progress, coins, stars, mascots and sound/haptic preferences stay on your device. No account, name, birth date, email or precise location is required. Reset Progress resets game data; uninstalling removes local app data. Resetting progress does not reset advertising privacy choices.

Optional advertising

The Shop offers optional Google AdMob video ads. Completing one earns 100 virtual coins; closing early earns no reward. Coins have no cash value. Gameplay remains available without watching ads. Requests use non-personalized advertising, a maximum G content rating, and disabled publisher first-party ID. This version does not ask for Apple's tracking permission.

SDK data processing

Non-personalized advertising does not mean no data collection. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and estimated general location, device-related identifiers, advertising views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, performance and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and SDK operation. Any advertising identifier availability remains controlled by iOS permissions. We do not provide Google with your name, email, birth date or game save.

Where required, Google's consent form appears before requesting ads. You can change available choices through Ad Privacy Choices in Settings. Depending on consent, region and inventory, ads may be limited or unavailable without affecting gameplay. A consent choice does not override this app's non-personalized request setting.

Read Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Google may process data outside your country under its safeguards and retention policies. We do not claim that resetting local progress deletes data held by Google.

Your choices and contact

Reset local game data in Settings or uninstall the app. Manage available advertising privacy choices in Settings and iOS permissions through system settings. For privacy questions or requests, use developer support without posting personal information. We do not intentionally solicit personal information from children. If personal information is inadvertently posted, request removal without repeating it publicly. This policy may change with app functionality.
