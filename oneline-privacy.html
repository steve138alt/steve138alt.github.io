OneLine Neon Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy describes the OneLine Neon iOS puzzle app.

Information kept on your device

OneLine Neon saves puzzle progress, daily puzzle completion, theme and sound preferences on your device. The app does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your puzzle progress. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control.

Optional rewarded ads

Each optional rewarded ad grants one puzzle hint. After three hints, another rewarded ad can reveal the complete route. Google AdMob provides these ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (and infer approximate location), device or advertising identifiers where available, ad views and interactions, crash and performance information to deliver, measure and improve advertising. Google may share data with advertising partners under its policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Where required, the app asks for advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. If Google requires a privacy options entry point for your region, you can reopen it in the app's Settings. You can continue playing without watching an ad; a hint is granted only when the ad reports a completed reward.

Children

OneLine Neon is a general audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly ask users to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising service, please contact us using the link below.

Your choices and contact

You can avoid optional ad requests by not choosing a rewarded hint. Device settings may provide additional advertising controls. To ask a privacy question or request assistance, email onelinesupport@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as the app or advertising services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
